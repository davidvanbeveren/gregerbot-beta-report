The error wall — share of responses mentioning “an error occurred”, in submission order

31-response rolling share. A contiguous block of the survey window — submissions ≈ #345–511 — hit errors on nearly every question.
Survey submissions in the order received → (no timestamps in the export; order is the best available time proxy)

What this looks like: a capacity or backend outage mid-beta, not user error — testers in that block got “an error occurred” on every question, simple or complex. Two details worth chasing: the error message itself is misspelled (“an error occured” — at least six testers called it out), and a tester in Singapore plus one asking “Only available in the US?” hint at possible geo-blocking. Separately, several sessions died after ~8–10 questions, which testers read as a hidden limit.

Method. 728 survey submissions to the GregerBot beta feedback form (“What did you think?” / “What problems did you encounter?” / “How could it be improved?”); 6 exact duplicate submissions removed, leaving 722. Themes were tagged with keyword rules and refined by manual review; a response can carry several themes, so theme counts sum to more than 722. Sentiment reflects each tester's overall verdict — the 82 responses with no usable verdict (blocked by errors, couldn't find the chat, or left the verdict blank) are excluded from the 97.7% positive rate. The survey export has no timestamps, so submission order stands in for time in the error-wall chart.
